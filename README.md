## The fine print that kills payouts (read this before you shop)

This is where most people lose money they were legitimately owed. These conditions are documented on TopCashback's own retailer pages and blog:

1. **Finish the purchase in the same tab.** Clicking through to the retailer, wandering off to another tab, and coming back later can break the tracking. The official advice: keep your cart and the purchase in one tab from click to confirmation.
2. **Start with an empty shopping cart.** Items added to a Best Buy cart *before* clicking through from the portal can be ineligible for cash back. Build your cart after the redirect, not before.
3. **Don't paste random coupon codes.** Codes from outside the portal can override the tracking link and void the cash back. Use codes hosted inside the portal — the Surfshark "discount code + cash back" listing is exactly this kind of sanctioned stack.
4. **Watch your browser.** On some retailer pages, Safari tracking isn't guaranteed; Chrome is the safe default. The browser extension (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) automates activation, but same-tab checkout still applies.
5. **File missing cash back claims within 30 days.** If a purchase doesn't track automatically, you have 30 days from the order to submit a Missing Cash Back Claim — and claims needing manual tracking may not count toward any sign-up bonus.
6. **No reselling.** Bulk purchases or purchases made with resale intent are ineligible and can be declined.

And one account-level rule that bites people months later: log in at least once every 6 months, or the account can be treated as dormant and terminated.
